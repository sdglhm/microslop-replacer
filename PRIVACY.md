

# **Privacy Policy – Microslop Replacer**

**Last updated:** 2026

Microslop Replacer is a Chrome browser extension designed to perform a simple, client-side text transformation on webpages viewed by the user.

This extension takes your privacy seriously.



## **Data Collection**

Microslop Replacer **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data**.

Specifically, the extension:

* Does **not** collect personal information
* Does **not** collect browsing history
* Does **not** collect user identifiers
* Does **not** collect cookies or authentication data
* Does **not** collect form input or keystrokes



## **How the Extension Works**

The extension runs as a **content script** within your browser and performs a local text replacement on visible webpage content. All processing occurs **entirely on your device**.

No data is sent to external servers, and no information leaves your browser.



## **Third-Party Services**

This extension:

* Does **not** use analytics services
* Does **not** use advertising services
* Does **not** integrate with third-party APIs
* Does **not** communicate with any external servers



## **Permissions**

The extension requests access to all websites (`<all_urls>`) solely to allow the text replacement feature to function consistently across webpages where the target text may appear.

This permission is **not** used to monitor user activity or collect data.



## **Data Sharing**

No data is shared with any third parties, organizations, or services because no data is collected.



## **User Control**

Users can:

* Enable or disable the extension at any time
* Remove the extension entirely through Chrome’s extension management page



## **Changes to This Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the current published version of the extension.
Because the repository is intentionally immutable, changes to the extension or this policy would only occur via a separate, new release.
